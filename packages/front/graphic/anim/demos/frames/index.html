Frames — frame-sequence primitive

A discrete graphic.frames sequence: eight code-generated regular polygons, one more side than the last (triangle → decagon) — a literal shape-morph-per-frame flip book, played/paused/scrubbed entirely through the frames surface.

Variant: … — Frame: … — …

Jump to frame:

  • Play: resumes the shared scheduler-driven loop (Frames#play).
  • Pause: freezes on the current frame (Frames#pause).
  • Frame buttons: jump directly (Frames#seek), no autoplay.
