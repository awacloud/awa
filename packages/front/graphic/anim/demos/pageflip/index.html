Pageflip — six-page book, page-curl fold

A small book built entirely through graphic.pageflip — six inline-generated, numbered pages, forward/back controls driving the real page-curl fold (graphic.timeline + graphic.tween on the shared scheduler). Closing or reloading mid-turn exercises Pageflip#dispose()'s in-flight-turn cancellation. The Previous/Next buttons disable themselves for the duration of a turn, so an overlapping click can never reach the underlying ignore-while-in-flight guard from the UI — that guard (and the zero-orphaned-timeline teardown) is instead exercised directly in tests/demos-smoke.integration.test.js's "pageflip rapid-turn leg".
