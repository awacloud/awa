svg3d — heightmap terrain, drag to look

A software-3D terrain (4 608 triangles, procedurally generated, a sea level) rendered entirely in SVG through the composed graphic.svg3d (dist/build/graphic-svg3d.*): face colours (olive valleys, green peaks, water at sea level, each face shaded), the camera standing at the centre at peak height. It orbits slowly until you drag the terrain; then the drag turns the camera. No Canvas.
